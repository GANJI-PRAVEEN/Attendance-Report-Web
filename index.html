Aim of the Project

This tool helps students calculate attendance percentages and plan how many classes they must attend to reach the minimum required attendance (e.g. 75%). It provides quick answers:

  • How many days you need to attend
  • What your attendance will be after X classes
  • Remaining working days until semester end
